Defense Guide

1. Ranking

When a preemptive Riichi lands, count your safe tiles before you protect the hand.One safe tile for every Turn left is enough to survive the hand.

1.1 Grades

Work down the list from the top.Only Genbutsu is absolute; everything below it is relative.

2. Reading

2.1 Honors

Honors cannot form a Ryanmen Wait, so only Shanpon and Tanki remain.With no copies seen, both waits are live. Past the middle of the hand that is close to a No-suji.Two copies seen cuts Shanpon to one shape; three copies leaves Tanki alone.

2.2 Suji

Suji only covers Ryanmen. Kanchan, Penchan, Tanki, and Shanpon stay live.4, 5, and 6 need both sides to become Naka-suji. One side alone is Kata-suji.1-2-3 and 7-8-9 have a single Suji side, so Kata-suji is all they ever get.The Suji of the Riichi declaration tile is worse. It was drawn right before the call, so the Wait may have changed on that tile.

2.3 Kabe

Count Kabe across your hand, the discards, and the indicator.With all four 3s gone, 1s and 2s cannot build a Ryanmen.Kabe also covers only Ryanmen. Kanchan and Tanki remain.

3. Folding

3.1 Order

Match the turns left against the safe tiles in hand first.Spend them freely and discard the safest tile you hold.Hold them back and you run out late, then draw into a No-suji.

3.2 Chase riichi

Against two Riichi players, only a tile that is Genbutsu to both is complete.With no overlap, look at honors and Kabe first.Suji passes one player only; to the other it is a No-suji.
