9.4 Dangerous tiles

With no genbutsu, honors, kabe, or suji, you choose among dangerous tiles. Even then, some are a little less dangerous.

1 and 9 are the least dangerous, and 4, 5, and 6 are the most.

The closer to the edge, the fewer wait shapes use the tile. Only a 23-man ryanmen waits on 1-man, but 5-man is waited on by 34-man and 67-man ryanmen and a 46-man kanchan. Tanki and shanpon remain regardless of number.
